About

Who

I’m Ed Muhlestein, a Principal Software Engineer based in Layton, Utah.

I have spent most of my career building reliable software for demanding environments — first in oil & gas interpretation systems at Landmark Graphics / Halliburton, then in government and defense-related work, and currently as a Principal Consultant creating financial and maintenance reporting solutions for the U.S. Air Force at Hill AFB.

What I Do

I design and develop full-stack web applications, primarily with Angular on the front end and Java / Spring on the back end, backed by Oracle or PostgreSQL. I’ve also worked extensively with C/C++, UNIX/Linux systems programming, desktop Java applications, and more recently with Docker, FastAPI, and Node.js.

I enjoy turning complex requirements into clear, actionable tools. A recent example is a set of financial reports I built for the Maintenance organization at Hill AFB that give leadership the visibility needed to make better spending decisions — with the potential to save tens of millions of dollars.

Background Highlights

  • Systems Implementers (2020–Present) — Principal Consultant / Principal Software Engineer. Full-stack Angular + Java/Spring solutions and financial reporting for the U.S. Air Force.
  • Halliburton (2014–2020) — Senior Technical Advisor. Front-end Angular microservices, full-stack Spring Boot + PostgreSQL work, and earlier Java desktop geologic interpretation tools. Contributed to features that supported major contracts and operational improvements.
  • ICON Information Consultants (2007–2014) — Software Consultant supporting Halliburton’s C/C++ geologic interpretation suite. Built tools that helped win customer decisions.
  • Hill Air Force Base (2006–2014) — Electrical Engineer (GS-12/13). Lead architect on redesign of a real-time F-16 avionics flight simulator.
  • Landmark Graphics — Principal Software Engineer. Long-term work on industry-leading geologic interpretation software; led the UNIX-to-Linux port.

Earlier experience includes C++ microkernel work on AIX at IBM and UNIX/Motif contract development.

Education & Certifications

  • B.S. Electrical Engineering, University of Utah
  • Completed approximately 50% of an M.S. in Computer Science
  • CompTIA Security+

Currently Exploring

Outside of work I’m building a personal microservices project (Node.js, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Docker Compose, Nginx) that is publicly available at edmuhlestein.com. The main goal is continuous learning; the secondary goal is to keep sharpening modern full-stack and container skills.

I’m always happy to talk about software architecture, government/enterprise systems, or new technologies.
